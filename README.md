# FreeHand brush strokes

Brush strokes are ways to make [FreeHand](https://github.com/TracySmithConsulting/freehand) your own, made and shared by
the people who use it. This is the gallery: every brush stroke here has been checked before it was listed.

A brush stroke is one of four kinds:

- **A team member:** a helper with one job, such as drafting posts.
- **A skill:** steps FreeHand can follow.
- **A voice pack:** how FreeHand sounds: its name, greeting, way of talking, and the reading-aloud voice it prefers.
- **A look:** FreeHand's colours and text size. Every colour is checked to be easy to read.

## Adding one to your FreeHand

1. In FreeHand, open **Brush strokes**. The gallery is listed there: choose **Look at it** next to the one you want.
   (Or paste the link to its folder here, for example
   `https://github.com/TracySmithConsulting/freehand-brush-strokes/tree/main/team-members/marketing-helper`.)
2. FreeHand checks it and shows you what it is, who made it, what it can use and its instructions. Nothing is added
   until you choose **Add it**.

A team member you add always asks you first before it sends, posts or changes anything. A skill starts as a draft, and
stays only if it works. A voice pack and a look change FreeHand only for you, and a new one replaces the old.
**Remove**, on the same page, takes any of them away again.

## Sharing yours

See [how to share a brush stroke](CONTRIBUTING.md). In short: in FreeHand, choose **Share as a brush stroke** on one of
your team members, then send it here as a pull request. It's listed once it has been checked.

## Only words and settings

A brush stroke is a `brush-stroke.json` that says what it is, and at most one file of words. It never contains a program, a
key, a password, an account or anyone's memories, and FreeHand refuses one that tries. That's what makes it safe to add
something a stranger made, and it's why each one is checked before it's listed here.
