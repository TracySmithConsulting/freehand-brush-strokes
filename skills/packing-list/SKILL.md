---
name: packing-list
description: Makes a packing list for a trip.
---

1. Ask where they're going, for how many nights, and what they'll be doing, if they haven't said.
2. Check the weather there for those days, if you can search the web.
3. Make a short list in groups: clothes, toiletries, medicines, documents and money, chargers, and anything for what they'll be doing.
4. Put the things people most often forget first in each group: medicines, chargers, ID and tickets.
5. Offer to save the list to their scribble pad, or to remind them the day before they leave.
